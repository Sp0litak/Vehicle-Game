# Vehicle Game

A Unity test assignment where a car automatically moves toward the finish while the player controls a turret and shoots enemies. The goal is to reach the finish before the car runs out of health.

## Getting Started

1. Open the project in Unity 6000.3.16f1.
2. Open `Assets/_Vehicle Game/Scenes/Game.unity`.
3. Press Play.

## Controls

- Click or tap to start.
- Hold to shoot.
- Drag horizontally while holding to rotate the turret.
- Click or tap after winning or losing to restart.

## Implementation

- Zenject for dependency injection and enemy and bullet pooling.
- NavMesh for enemy movement.
- DOTween for turret recoil animation.
- UI displaying health, route progress, and remaining distance.
- Bullet trails, a laser sight, and object destruction effects.
